# Bruschetta

Vegan and bright — tomato, garlic, basil, olive oil on toasted bread.

## Ingredients

- {4 slices} crusty bread
- {3 tomatoes}, diced
- {2 cloves} garlic
- {6 leaves} fresh basil
- {2 tablespoons} olive oil
- {1 pinch} salt

## Method

1. Toast the bread.
2. Mix diced tomatoes with finely chopped basil, olive oil, salt.
3. Rub the toasted bread with garlic, top with the tomato mix.
