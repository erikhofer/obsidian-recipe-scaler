# Chocolate Avocado Mousse

Silky, rich, and accidentally vegan.

## Ingredients

- {2 avocados}, ripe
- {4 tablespoons} cocoa powder
- {4 tablespoons} maple syrup
- {100 ml} plant milk
- {1 teaspoon} vanilla extract
- {1 pinch} salt

## Method

1. Blend everything until smooth.
2. Chill {1 hour} before serving.
