# Pasta Aglio e Olio

Five ingredients, twenty minutes, deeply satisfying.

## Ingredients

- {400 g} spaghetti
- {6 cloves} garlic, thinly sliced
- {80 ml} olive oil
- {1 pinch} chili flakes
- {2 tablespoons} chopped parsley
- {1 teaspoon} salt

## Method

1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente.
2. Warm olive oil with garlic and chili over low heat until garlic turns pale gold.
3. Toss drained pasta with the oil, finish with parsley.
